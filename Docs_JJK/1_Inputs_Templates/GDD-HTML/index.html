01 [領域展開] PURPOSE & PRODUCTION CONSTRAINTS

Neo-JJK shifts away from the complexity of traditional action fighters. We target a modular **2D Side-Scrolling Hack-and-Slash Rogue-lite** focused entirely on casual entry barriers, minimal script footprint, and supreme visual payoffs.

Our workflow rules demand strict encapsulation. Scripts must remain short, modular, and performance-tuned. Development prioritization favors flashy graphical representation (VFX) over highly authored animation sequences.

CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS
  • ▶ Low dev effort: Minimal custom scripts.
  • ▶ Low skill floor/ceiling: Casual friendly.
  • ▶ Visual emphasis: Large VFX slash arcs.
  • ▶ Simple logic: SO driven properties.
