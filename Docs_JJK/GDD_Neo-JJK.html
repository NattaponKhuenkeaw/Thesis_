01 [領域展開] PURPOSE & PRODUCTION CONSTRAINTS

Neo-JJK shifts away from the complexity of traditional action fighters. We target a modular **2D Side-Scrolling Hack-and-Slash Rogue-lite** focused entirely on casual entry barriers, minimal script footprint, and supreme visual payoffs.

Our workflow rules demand strict encapsulation. Scripts must remain short, modular, and performance-tuned. Development prioritization favors flashy graphical representation (VFX) over highly authored animation sequences.

CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS
  • ▶ Low dev effort: Minimal custom scripts.
  • ▶ Low skill floor/ceiling: Casual friendly.
  • ▶ Visual emphasis: Large VFX slash arcs.
  • ▶ Simple logic: SO driven properties.

06 [戦闘技術] ROTATION COMBAT VS LEGACY DNA

We have stripped complex fighter frame data and parry mechanics, replacing them with skill-cooldown rotations and simple dash-invincibility windows.

Standard Combat Features

  • Simple Hit Reactions: Short stagger, visual knockback effects.
  • Swap Cancel: Active actions continue during character swaps.
  • iFrame Dodge: Short invincibility frames on dodge dash.
  • Hit-Stop Freezer: Freezes visual elements on landed criticals.

Team Role Breakdown

  • Main DPS: High-field presence, executes core combos.
  • Sub DPS: Dumps skill/ultimate cooldowns, exits field.
  • Support: Offfield energy regen, shields, heals.

Removed Core Systems

  • ✖ Complex branching combo trees (e.g. BlazBlue style).
  • ✖ Millisecond active cancel frame windows.
  • ✖ Physics-based airborne juggles & gravity state rules.
  • ✖ Perfect swap counters and timing checks.

Removed Hit States

  • ✖ Long air- juggle locks and launch physics loops.
  • ✖ Stagger-lock loops against Special Grade bosses.
  • ✖ Damage Scaling governed by overall combo counters.

10 [大敵決戦] PATTERN-BASED BOSS FIGHT ENGINE

Subway Bosses act as large-scale combat puzzles. Players dodge incoming hazard shapes, then swap characters to deploy heavy bursts during vulnerability states.

Standard Boss Mechanics

  • ✔ Large silhouettes: High placement and visual readability.
  • ✔ Telegraphed warnings: Danger outlines match spatial hits.
  • ✔ Stagger resistance: Armor is bypassable only during stun states.
  • ✔ Phase shifts: Combat scales as HP boundaries fall below 60% and 30%.

Banned Boss Behaviors

  • ✖ No launcher grab states or infinite combo juggle targets.
  • ✖ No frame-advantage trap locks.
  • ✖ No dynamic input-reading dodge maneuvers.
